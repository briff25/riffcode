About

Merchandising, store operations, finance and technology all meet on the sales floor. That's where I work.

For more than twenty years I've turned floor plans, planograms and sales data into decisions executives can act on. At Big Lots I directed macro space planning and analytics across 1,400 stores and led the Blue Yonder (JDA) Category Management implementation from RFP and vendor selection through organizational redesign, testing, training and rollout.

Before that I built out corporate floor planning at Dick's Sporting Goods as the chain grew from 125 to 450 stores, and ran store design and fixture procurement at PetPeople while it expanded from 42 to 74 locations.

Now I'm pointing that experience at AI. For a Fortune 500 retailer's new-store program, I build custom GPTs that turn scattered source data into submission-ready purchase orders, and they've cut procurement cycle time by 75%. I studied philosophy and mathematics before my MBA, and I still think a good space plan is a good argument: clear premises, honest numbers, and a conclusion you can defend in front of the CFO.
