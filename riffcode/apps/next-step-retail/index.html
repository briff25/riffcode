Our Service Portfolio

📐

Floor Plan Creation

Ensures each new store "feels like the brand" and supports sales per square foot goals. Design or review traffic flow, product adjacencies, decompression zones, and line-of-sight pathways using strategic zoning logic.

Deliverables:

  • AutoCAD floor plans
  • Space plans integrating back-of-house needs
  • Traffic flow optimization
  • Strategic product placement zones
🛠️

Fixture Selection

Recommend fixture packages based on product mix, aesthetics, and inventory capacity. Oversee delivery timelines and installation coordination for seamless implementation.

Deliverables:

  • Comprehensive fixture spec sheets
  • Detailed sourcing lists
  • Installation timeline coordination
  • Quality assurance oversight
🎨

Visual Merchandising Setup

Develop visual merchandising guidelines and training materials for staff. Create or execute floor sets and displays that drive sales and enhance customer experience.

Deliverables:

  • Custom planograms and photo references
  • Opening-week VM checklists
  • Staff training decks and materials
  • On-site coaching and support
⚙️

Cross-Functional Coordination

Expert project management acting as liaison between operations, visual, marketing, IT, and construction teams. Keep new store openings on schedule, budget, and brand.

Deliverables:

  • Detailed project timelines
  • Comprehensive punch lists
  • Regular status updates
  • Cross-team communication management
